1. Summary

Gap Calculator works on your device. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise location. The numbers you enter into the calculator are not sent to us. The app shows ads and uses basic analytics, which are provided by Google and described below.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves two small settings on your device only: your selected language and whether you have seen the welcome tour. This stays on your device and is removed if you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

3. Information collected by third-party services

The app includes these Google services. They may collect the information listed, which can be linked to your device but not to your name, as described in Google’s own policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising). Shows banner ads. May collect your advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system information, app interaction and diagnostic data, and approximate location derived from your IP address. This is used to serve, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. Learn more: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google). Collects app-usage and device information (for example an app-instance identifier, app version, device model, operating system, country and general usage events) so we can understand how the app is used and fix problems. Learn more: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Google Play services / Play In-App Updates. Checks whether a newer version of the app is available on Google Play.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for automated decisions that have legal effects on you.

4. Consent and your choices

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: when you first open the app you will be asked for your consent before ads and ad-related data collection start. You can change your choice later using the “Privacy settings” link at the bottom of the app.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Analytics: you can stop analytics by using the consent choice above or by uninstalling the app.

5. Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

For users in the EEA/UK we rely on your consent for advertising and for storing or accessing information on your device that is not strictly necessary. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing.

6. Sharing and international transfers

Data collected by Google services is processed by Google and may be transferred to and stored in countries outside your own, including the United States, under safeguards that Google describes in its documentation (such as Standard Contractual Clauses). We do not otherwise share data with anyone.

7. Retention

We do not hold personal data ourselves. Retention of data collected by AdMob and Firebase is controlled by Google under its policies and settings.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) you may have the right to access, correct, erase or restrict use of your personal data, to withdraw consent, to object, and to complain to your data-protection authority. Because we do not hold data that identifies you, requests about data collected by Google services are best exercised through your Google account’s privacy controls. You can also contact us at the address above and we will help as far as we can.

9. Children

The app is a general-purpose tool for measuring and spacing and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where a higher age applies). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help get it removed.

10. Security

The calculator runs locally and does not need an internet connection. All network connections made by the app’s third-party services use encryption in transit.
